# Superstore-Sales
SUPERSTORE SALES DASHBOARD

# Project Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Superstore sales performance across three regions—
1. Central 
2. East 
3. West.
It presents key metrics such as
  Total Sales, 
  Profit,
  Total Quantity Sold,
  Average Sales, 
  Profit Margin.
 The dashboard is interactive and built for dynamic filtering by region, sub-category, and year.

 # Objective
To visualize and analyze Superstore’s sales data to:
Identify high-performing regions and product categories.
Understand year-over-year sales trends.
Provide a quick executive overview of business performance.
Support strategic planning and inventory decisions.

# Tools & Technologies
Power BI
DAX for calculated metrics

# RegionaL Sales Performance
East: 138.94K (36.3%) – Top-performing region
Central: 132.58K (34.6%)
West: 111.56K (29.1%)
Insight: The East region generates the most revenue, suggesting a strong customer base or effective sales strategy.

# # 🗓️ Sales By Year

- **2020:** 91K
- **2021:** 91K
- **2022:** 110K
- **2023:** 11K (Likely partial data or decline)

**Insight:** Steady growth until 2022, followed by a sharp drop in 2023—possibly due to data cutoff or market shifts.

# Sales By Category
Office Supplies: 141K – Highest overall category sales
Furniture: 132K
Technology: 117K
Insight: Office Supplies dominate sales volume, while Furniture and Technology are strong performers.

# Key Insight
The East region is the strongest in sales, indicating an opportunity for deeper investment or marketing.
Office Supplies lead in volume, but other categories may offer higher profit margins.
The 2023 sales drop requires further analysis—it may be due to incomplete data or external challenges.
The average sales value of 255.39 suggests potential for upselling or bundling.
